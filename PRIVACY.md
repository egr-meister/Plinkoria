# Plinkoria — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 14, 2026_

Plinkoria is a fully offline casual game (Quiz and Matching Pairs).

- **No data collection.** The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or device data.
- **No internet access.** The app does not request the `INTERNET` permission and has no network code.
- **No accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or attribution SDKs.**
- **Local storage only.** Quiz best scores, cleared Matching Pairs levels and the sound on/off setting are stored on your device using Android DataStore. They may be included in your device's own Android backup if you have it enabled.
- **Deleting data.** Use *Settings → Reset Progress*, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app.
- **Children.** The app contains no data collection of any kind, including from children.
- **External links.** "Rate" and "Full policy" open the Google Play Store or your browser — those services have their own policies.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/egr-meister/Plinkoria/issues
